Questions from property owners

Ask About Management {{arrow}}

Do you manage long-term and short-term rentals?

We provide support for both rental types. The services and responsibilities are discussed for each property and recorded in the management agreement.

Where do you provide services?

We focus on East Central Minnesota. Send us the property location so we can confirm whether it falls within our service area.

What does property management cost?

Fees depend on the property and agreed services. Request a quote so we can review your needs and explain the applicable fees.

Who pays for repairs?

The agreement explains maintenance responsibilities, charges, and owner approval requirements. We review those details before management begins.

Can you help if my rental is vacant?

Tell us about the vacancy, property condition, and intended availability date. We can discuss rental marketing and leasing support.
